Set up two-factor

Scan this with an authenticator app, then type the six-digit code it shows.

No email is sent. Your codes are generated on your phone by the authenticator app, and change every 30 seconds. There is nothing to wait for in your inbox.

I don't have an authenticator app

Install a free one from your phone's app store — Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator and Authy all work, as do password managers like 1Password and Bitwarden. Open it, choose "scan a QR code", and point it at the square below.
