How it works

  1. 1
    Sign up with your university email

    Your address decides which university you join. Teaching staff add a code from their department to be able to create classrooms.

  2. 2
    Teachers open a classroom

    Private by default. A short join code — the kind you can read out — is what lets students in. Rotate it whenever enrolment should close.

  3. 3
    Everything for the course lives in one place

    Seven tabs per classroom: Content, Calendar, Announcements, Discussions, Quiz, Gradebook and Messages.

Built for institutions

Separate ecosystems

A classroom at one university is invisible to every other. Join codes and email addresses never collide across institutions.

Two-factor by default

Every account is protected by an authenticator app. No codes are ever emailed or texted, so there is nothing to intercept.

Roles that match reality

A graduate student can teach one classroom and take another. Roles belong to the enrolment, not the person.

Quizzes that mark themselves

Timed quizzes with automatic marking, carried over from SegueQuiz, feeding straight into the gradebook.
